# H1 — intraday 0DTE variance risk premium (selling defined-risk 0DTE structures)

Written 2026-11-15, before any option or stock price was read for this test. The only data looked at beforehand: which dates have a same-day expiration in the option files (SPY and QQQ: 562 of 563 development days, 2024-02-02 missing for both; IWM: 541 of 563, no 0DTE on most Tuesdays/Thursdays before 2024-04-22), the list of early-close days, and that the stock files contain a 16:00 bar.

## Hypothesis and who is on the other side

The ~1,100 long-option configs in `../LOG.md` lost money almost everywhere (median t ≈ −2), even ones with no directional skill should only lose the spread. That pattern suggests intraday option buyers overpay for movement. If so, the other side, selling 0DTE premium and holding, should earn a positive return after costs.

- **Who pays:** intraday hedgers buying protection for the rest of the day, retail lottery demand for cheap 0DTE options, and directional traders who pay for convexity.
- **Why it could persist:** sellers carry a short-gamma tail (a large intraday move can cost many days of premium), and market makers need to be paid for warehousing it.
- **Why it might not:** 0DTE is now the most heavily traded segment, with lots of systematic sellers. Four legs pay four spreads, and the edge per day may be smaller than those costs.

## Rule (primary config, P)

For each of SPY, QQQ and IWM, on each development trading day with a same-day expiration in the option file:

1. **Entry time T = 10:00 NY.** Spot S = close of the last 1-minute stock bar starting before T (the 09:59 bar).
2. **Strikes.** ATM strike K = S rounded to the nearest whole dollar (x.50 rounds up). Wing width W = max($1, round(1% × S)) in whole dollars, set from that day's S by this formula (SPY ≈ $5–7, QQQ ≈ $4–6, IWM ≈ $2).
3. **Iron butterfly:** sell the 0DTE call and put at K, buy the 0DTE call at K + W and the put at K − W. One contract each, one structure per ticker per day.
4. **Fills (README model, unchanged):** each leg fills on the open of the first 1-minute bar of that contract starting at or after T, if one starts by T + 5 minutes. Trade price = mid; spread = max(minSpread, spreadPct × price); sells get mid − half rounded down to the cent, buys pay mid + half rounded up. Fee $0.05 per contract per side, so $0.20 per structure to open. **If any leg has no bar in the window, the whole structure is skipped** (no fallback strike), and the skip is counted.
5. **Hold to expiry.** Each leg settles at intrinsic value against the underlying's official close = close of the stock bar starting at the session close (16:00, or 13:00 on early-close days; if that bar is missing, the last bar before it). No spread and no fee at expiry.
6. **P&L per structure** = 100 × (credit received − settlement value of the four legs) − fees. **Max loss** = 100 × W − 100 × credit + fees (the per-structure risk; reported in dollars and P&L as a fraction of max loss).

## Cost levels (all reported)

| Level   | spreadPct | minSpread | fee/contract/side |
| ------- | --------- | --------- | ----------------- |
| Primary | 1%        | $0.01     | $0.05             |
| Stress  | 2%        | $0.01     | $0.05             |
| Harsh   | 1%        | $0.04     | $0.05             |

The grid and the leg selection do not depend on the cost level; only the fill prices do.

## Grid (robustness, not search): 19 configs, counted

Core grid, 16 configs = entry time {09:45, 10:00, 12:00, 14:00} × structure {iron butterfly, iron condor} × exit {hold to expiry, take profit at 50%}.

- **Iron condor:** short call at round(1.005 × S), short put at round(0.995 × S) (if either rounds onto K or the wrong side of S, push it one strike out so the short call is > S and short put < S), wings W further out (same W formula).
- **Take profit at 50% (TP50):** at the close of each 1-minute bar m from entry until 15:50 (12:50 on early-close days), mark each leg at its latest trade close at or before m (stale wings keep their last print). If the debit to close at those marks is ≤ 50% of the credit received at entry (after costs), a closing order is decided at the end of minute m. Each leg then fills on the first bar starting after m (i.e. at or after m + 1 minute, within 5 minutes) with modeled costs and fees. "Never better than the trigger": the closing debit at mid is floored at the triggering mark debit before costs are added. A leg that can't fill in the window stays open and settles at intrinsic at expiry. If never triggered, hold to expiry.
- **Entry 14:00** is skipped on early-close days (the market closes at 13:00).

Extra neighbours of P, 3 configs:

- P with wing width 0.5% (W = max(1, round(0.5% × S))) and 2% (W = max(1, round(2% × S))): 2 configs.
- P but close all four legs at 15:50 (12:50 on early-close days) instead of holding to expiry, each leg on its first print within 5 minutes; a leg that can't fill settles at intrinsic from the official close minus the modeled half-spread (the engine's rule). 1 config. This is the realistic way to avoid assignment and after-hours pin risk.

Total 19 configs. No others will be run on development data without a dated note below that counts them.

## Statistics

- **Daily P&L** = sum over tickers of that day's structures (0, 1, 2 or 3). All t-stats are on daily P&L across development days with at least one structure.
- Reported for each config and cost level: structures, skips (and why), net $, mean per day, daily t, per ticker (net, t), per year (2024 Feb–Dec, 2025, 2026 Jan–Apr), win rate, worst day, max drawdown of cumulative daily P&L, both also divided by the average daily credit collected, net without the 5 best days, mean P&L / max loss.
- **Leg data quality (P):** fill rate per leg type and ticker, fill delay after T (0–5 minutes), volume of the fill bar, and how often the four prints are inconsistent: a wing priced above its short leg, or the mid credit ≥ W or ≤ 0 (a stale print). Result excluding inconsistent structures is information only.
- **Unhedged benchmark (information only):** at 10:00, the ATM straddle price (call + put fill prints, no costs) against the realised |close − S| and the straddle's payoff |close − K|, mean and ratio, per ticker.

## Pass rule (development, config P)

All of:

1. Net > 0 at primary and at stress costs.
2. Daily-P&L t ≥ 2.5 at primary costs.
3. Net > 0 in each development year (2024 Feb–Dec, 2025, 2026 Jan–Apr) at primary costs.
4. Net > 0 on at least 2 of 3 tickers at primary costs.
5. Net > 0 at primary costs without the 5 best days.
6. Net ≥ 0 at harsh costs.
7. Neighbours agree: at least 8 of the 16 core-grid configs net > 0 at primary costs.

Worst day and max drawdown relative to average daily credit are always reported; they are not part of the rule but are the main thing to read for a short-vol trade.

## Holdout (only if P passes)

Frozen candidate: P only, one candidate; no best-of-grid neighbour is added. Run once on 2026-05-01 → 2026-11-13. Holdout pass: net > 0 on ≥ 2 of 3 tickers, daily t ≥ 2, and net ≥ 0 at stress costs.

### Leg data quality (P)

Each of the four legs had a print within 5 minutes on 98.9–99.6% of ticker-days. Over 98.6% of fills were in the 10:00 bar itself, and at most 2.3% of fill bars had 2 contracts or fewer (SPY call wing). 14 of 1,665 ticker-days were skipped for a missing leg. Only 2 structures had inconsistent prints (wing above its short leg, or credit ≥ width); IWM 2024-08-05 is one. Excluding them: +$12,837, t 1.54. At 1% wings and a 10:00 entry, stale or missing wings are not a problem. They are for the iron condor later in the day: at 14:00, 575 of 1,665 condors were skipped because a wing ~1.5% out had no print.

### Dated note, 2026-11-15, after the run: a data-availability look-ahead (does not affect P)

The option files hold strikes from $10 below the day's low to $10 above the day's high (`src/download/download.ts`, using the whole day's bars). A strike more than $10 from the entry spot is therefore only on disk if the rest of the day moved toward it. P's legs are always within $7.50 of spot, so P is unaffected. ### Information-only checks (added after seeing the results; counted below)

1. **Settlement on the 16:00 bar's open instead of its close.** In July–August 2025 (no ex-dividend date), the ratio of the official daily close to the 16:00 bar's open is constant to within 7×10⁻⁵ for SPY and IWM (1.2×10⁻⁴ for QQQ). The ratio to the 16:00 bar's close drifts four times as much (3–4×10⁻⁴), because the bar's close includes after-hours prints. So the closing-auction print is the bar's open, and the README's "16:00 bar close" is slightly off. P settled on the open: +$10,160, t 1.21 (harsh +$4,137, t 0.49). IB 14:00: +$32,678, t 5.48 (no change).
2. **Worst print in the fill bar.** Each sell fills at the fill bar's low and each buy at its high, then the modeled spread is applied. This is an extreme bound, and it mixes the spread with a minute of price movement. P: −$45,218 (t −5.15). IB 12:00: +$5,821 (t 0.78). IB 14:00: +$6,852 (t 1.12), +$969 at harsh costs. With both checks 1 and 2: IB 14:00 +$7,083 (t 1.15).
3. **Closing all legs at 15:50 instead of expiry**, for the afternoon entries (2 new configs): IB 12:00 +$6,106 (t 1.00), −$3,601 at harsh costs. IB 14:00 +$3,422 (t 0.79), −$5,857 at harsh costs. ### Reading it

- **The trade-print data do show an intraday variance premium.** At 10:00, 0DTE straddles cost 6–10% more than they paid out, and at 14:00 about 20% more. Shorting the 10:00 iron butterfly earns it, but too little to pass after four spreads: t 1.5, IWM negative, t 0.8 at harsh costs. The pre-registered test fails.
- **The afternoon entries look much stronger (t 3.9 at 12:00, 5.4 at 14:00, across tickers and years), but three things undercut them.**
  - They were not predicted: they're the best rows of a 16-config grid.
  - Nearly all of the profit needs holding through the last ten minutes to the settlement print. Closing at 15:50 leaves t < 1. Expiring SPY/QQQ/IWM options trade until 16:15 and are physically settled, so holding to expiry means assignment and after-hours pin risk that this model doesn't charge for.
  - The result depends on trade prints being the midpoint. Under the worst-print bound, the 14:00 edge falls to t ≈ 1.1, and to about zero at harsh costs.
- To take the afternoon effect further, it would need a new pre-registered test with real bid/ask quotes, or at least a few weeks of live quote checks at 14:00 and 15:50, before the holdout is spent on it.
