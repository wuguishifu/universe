# H18: the H17 pre-market box signal expressed in QQQ options (written 2026-11-15, before any run on option prices)

Goal: keep the H17 strategy tradeable at Alpaca, which has no futures. H17 (QQQ as an NQ proxy) passed its pre-registered rule at +0.139R, t 2.99, on 2016–2026. H18 asks whether the same signal survives being traded through options at real NBBO quotes.

Prior: low to moderate. In 0DTE, a long option pays implied volatility, which is usually rich, and the signal's edge is small: about +6 bp of the underlying per trade. LOG idea 4 (buying 0DTE options on opening-range breaks, 2024-02 → 2026-04) found nothing (best t 1.1 of 216 configs), but it used trade bars and a different signal.

## Signal (unchanged from H17 config P)

The box is 09:00–09:30, built from 1-minute bars (≥ 20 bars). The breakout is the first 5-minute close outside the box, from 09:30 to 10:30. The filter is the 15-minute EMA 50/200 over 04:00–20:00 bars. The trade enters at the open of the next minute. It exits when the underlying hits the far side of the box (stop), reaches 3R (target), or at 15:55 (flat). This is the H17 code, copied into `tools/h18-options.mjs`.

## Option fills (real quotes, `data/quotes/QQQ`)

- **Entry:** the NBBO standing at the entry minute (the same minute the H17 share entry uses). Buys pay the ask and sells get the bid. Skip the trade if any leg has no quote then, or a non-positive ask.
- **Exit:** taken when the underlying event happens.
  - If the stop or target is hit inside 1-minute bar m, exit at the quote standing at m+1. That is the earliest moment the bar is known to have ended.
  - A gap through the stop at the open of bar m exits at m.
  - Flat exits at 15:55, or 5 minutes before an early close.
  - Every leg is closed at its quote: sell longs at the bid, buy back shorts at the ask. Nothing is held to expiry.
- **Fee:** $0.05 per contract per side (as H1).
- **Costs:**
  - primary = the real NBBO;
  - stress = $0.01 worse per leg per side;
  - harsh = $0.02 worse.
- **Strike look-ahead:** files only hold strikes within $10 of each day's range. Skip a trade if any chosen strike is more than $10 from the entry spot, and count the skips.

## Expressions (grid: 7 configs; count all)

The expiration is the nearest one on or after the trade date (0DTE) unless stated otherwise. Long signals use calls, and short signals use puts. The credit spread does the reverse, on the side away from the move.

| id | Structure | Strikes |
|---|---|---|
| A | long option | ATM: the strike nearest the entry spot |
| B | long option, next expiration after today | ATM |
| C | long option, ITM | the strike nearest the stop level (box far side): about 1R ITM |
| D | debit vertical | long ATM, short the strike nearest the 3R target |
| E | credit vertical, away from the move | short the first strike beyond the stop (≤ box low for longs), long $2 further out |
| F | A, but ignore the underlying stop (option premium is the stop) | ATM |
| G | A, entry one minute late (quote at entry + 1) | ATM |

Each config has one trade per signal day.

## Measures

The primary measure is **return on risk** per trade: P&L divided by the maximum possible loss. That's the debit for A, B, C, D, F and G, and the width − credit for E. Fees are included. I'll also report $ per contract, win rate, t (one trade per day, so trade t = daily t), max drawdown in units of risk, per-year results, and the H17 share result (in R) on the same days for comparison.

## Samples

Development is 2024-02-01 → 2026-04-30. Holdout is 2026-05-01 → 2026-11-13, run once on at most 2 candidates frozen in writing below the development results. Caveat: the H17 signal itself was already run over the holdout dates (as shares), so the holdout only tests the choice of option expression, not the signal.

## Pass rule

**Candidate (development, primary cost):** mean return on risk > 0 with t ≥ 2.0, and mean > 0 at stress cost. If there are more than 2 candidates, freeze the 2 with the highest t. F and G are information only and can't be candidates.

**H18 PASS:** a frozen candidate has holdout mean > 0 at primary cost and at stress cost. FAIL if no candidate exists, or none is > 0 in the holdout.

**Verdict: PASS** (both frozen candidates > 0 at primary and stress cost in the holdout).

**What this does and doesn't show:**
- It shows that, given the H17 signal, buying an ATM QQQ option and exiting on the underlying's stop, target or flat keeps the signal's edge at real quotes and spreads. Theta and spread don't eat it, because most trades are stopped within the first hour, and QQQ ATM spreads are a cent or two.
- It does not independently confirm the signal. H17's own run already covered these holdout dates, and the video's author chose the rules on 2016–2026. Every caveat in h17.md still applies, most of all that SPY and IWM show nothing.
- The holdout is only 68 trades (t ≈ 1.7). Its sign agrees; its size is noise.
- The underlying stop is essential. F (holding the option with no stop) drops to t 0.7.
